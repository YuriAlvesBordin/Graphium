10 · Lifecycle & Destroy (SPA)

Cleanup to prevent memory leaks in single-page applications. Call destroy() when the view unmounts: it disconnects the ResizeObserver, stops the run loop, frees the WebGL resources and clears every listener.

state: mounted · 0 nodes

Simulates a SPA route: mount creates the instance and subscribes to events; unmount calls destroy() and drops the canvas. Listeners registered through graph.on() die with the instance — nothing leaks between mounts.
